## Required before marking the record verified

- [ ] Locate the official court docket and primary order, or document why primary access is unavailable.
- [ ] Retrieve and read the order and relevant underlying motion/complaint.
- [ ] Review subsequent docket entries for modification, vacatur, stay, discharge, or disposition.
- [ ] Confirm exact wallet/address scope directly from primary documents.
- [ ] Distinguish a government allegation/request from a court finding or operative order.
- [ ] Record retrieval timestamp, source URL, file hash, reviewer, and verification rationale.
- [ ] Record unresolved questions and the next review date.

## Evidence handling rules

- A party or community submission is not proof.
- AI-generated matches and news/social references are discovery leads, not authoritative verification.
- A cryptographic hash establishes that a file matches a known byte sequence; it does not establish authorship, authenticity, truth, or legal effect.
- A timestamp or on-chain hash commitment can support a claim about the existence of a commitment by a time, but does not prove when the original web page first appeared.
- “No record found” must never be shown as “legally clear.”
- Preserve source and version history instead of silently replacing prior records.

## Product disclosure

The application is an information and evidence workflow, not a court, adjudicator, legal opinion, or transaction-blocking mechanism. It must not automatically label an asset unlawful merely because a dispute is reported.
